Every time you open a web page, one computer asks another for something, and the other one answers. That's the whole trick. Everything else (domain names, servers, proxies, padlocks, clouds) is armour bolted on over the years.

This book takes the armour off, one piece at a time. You'll type real commands, read real conversations between machines, and by the end you'll be able to put your own corner of the web online and understand every layer standing in front of it.
